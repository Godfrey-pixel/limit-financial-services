About Limit Financial

Professional payday lending backed by real industry experience.

Our Mission

Limit Financial Services provides accessible, transparent, and responsible payday loans to South Africans. Founded by industry veterans who saw the need for a modern, digital-first lender that prioritises customer welfare and regulatory compliance. We believe everyone deserves access to emergency funds — but only when it is affordable and sustainable.

Who We Are

Limit Financial Services (Pty) Ltd is a registered credit provider based in Bloemfontein, regulated by the National Credit Regulator (NCR). Our founding team brings over a decade of combined experience in the South African micro lending industry, having previously held senior roles at established financial institutions. We launched Limit Financial to bring the same professional standards and responsible lending practices to a modern, fully digital platform.

Responsible Lending

With deep roots in the micro lending industry, our team understands the importance of responsible lending. Every application undergoes a credit check and affordability assessment. We never lend more than a customer can afford to repay on their next payday. Our processes are built on years of industry experience and a commitment to treating every customer fairly.

Compliance

Limit Financial is fully compliant with the National Credit Act, 2005 (Act 34 of 2005), the Consumer Protection Act, and the Protection of Your Details Act (POPIA). We are members of the Micro Finance South Africa (MFSA) association.

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information including your name, ID number, contact details, employment information, banking details, and financial information. This is necessary to process your loan application and perform credit and affordability checks.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to: process loan applications, perform credit checks with registered credit bureaus, assess affordability, communicate with you about your account, comply with legal obligations, and improve our services.

3. Credit Bureau Reporting

We report loan information to registered credit bureaus (TransUnion, Experian, XDS) as required by the National Credit Act. This includes application data, account performance, and payment history.

4. Data Security

We use 256-bit SSL encryption, secure servers, and strict access controls to protect your information. We never sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights (POPIA)

Under POPIA, you have the right to: access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements), object to processing, and lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for 5 years after account closure as required by the National Credit Act and Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA).

7. Contact Us

For privacy-related queries, email po@limitfinancial.co.za or write to our Information Officer at 45 President Steyn Avenue, Bloemfontein, 9301.

Terms & Conditions

Please read carefully before applying.

1. Agreement

By submitting an application, you agree to these Terms and Conditions. This constitutes a binding agreement between you ("the Borrower") and Limit Financial Services (Pty) Ltd ("the Lender").

2. Eligibility

You must be: a South African citizen or permanent resident, 18 years or older, employed or earning a regular income, and have a valid South African bank account. Approval is subject to credit and affordability assessments.

3. Loan Product

This is a payday loan product. The loan amount (R500 to R8,000) is disbursed to your bank account and must be repaid in full on your next payday, typically within 30 days. The total repayment consists of the loan amount plus a single flat fee of 30% of the loan amount.

4. Fees

Flat Fee: 30% of the loan amount (e.g., R900 on a R3,000 loan). There are no additional initiation fees, service fees, or monthly admin fees. All fees are NCR-compliant and disclosed before you sign.

5. Repayment

The full repayment amount (loan + 30% fee) is collected via debit order from your nominated bank account on your next payday. Late payments incur penalty fees as permitted by the National Credit Act.

6. Default

If you default on payment, we may: charge additional fees as permitted by law, report the default to credit bureaus, engage debt collection services, or take legal action. We encourage you to contact us immediately if you are experiencing financial difficulty.

7. Early Settlement

You may repay your loan early at any time without penalty. Contact us to request a settlement figure. You are entitled to settle early in terms of Section 125 of the National Credit Act.

8. Cooling-Off Period

In terms of Section 121 of the National Credit Act, you have the right to rescind a credit agreement within 5 business days of signing, provided you repay all amounts owed.

9. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa. Disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the South African courts.
