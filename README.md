# Limit Financial Services

> South Africa's trusted payday lending partner.

## About

Limit Financial Services (Pty) Ltd is an NCR-registered credit provider offering transparent, responsible payday loans from R500 to R8,000. Borrow up to R8,000 and repay in full on your next payday with a single flat fee of 30%.

All applications are subject to credit checks and affordability assessments as required by the National Credit Act.

## Fee Structure

| Fee | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| Flat Fee | 30% of loan amount |
| Example: R3,000 loan | R900 fee, R3,900 total repayment |

No initiation fees. No service fees. No monthly admin fees. One flat fee, disclosed upfront.
